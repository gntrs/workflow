# a filled taste file, one person's picks

an example of what a taste file looks like after a few weeks of real reactions. these picks are one person's, not rules: yours starts empty and fills with your own words. the file lives in the brain if installed, else `.claude/taste.md` in the repo.

```markdown
# taste

## things you have loved

- a notes app's sidebar: "calm, nothing shouts, i can find stuff"
- the landing we shipped in august: "this, exactly"

## things you have hated

- the first dashboard round: "feels generated. make it look like a tool a pro would pay for"
- a hero with a glow behind glass cards: "every generated site looks like this"

## refs

| date | ref | what it was for | your word | measured values |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-30 | a keyboard maker's product page | specs section | "this, but ours" | rows 48px, hairline alpha 0.08, mono labels 12px |

## type and color

- no purple to blue gradients. "that's the default look, i want ours"
- one flat orange accent, never a gradient of it: "keep the orange clean"

## mascots and icons

- mascots: no clip art. one soft shape, one flat color, pill eyes, alive (blinks, follows the pointer), shown in two or three directions to pick from. never a hand drawn character with outlines. never off screen, never over text.
- icons: one outline set on controls only. a licensed web set, never a platform set that is not licensed for the web.

## previews

- previews and social cards are 4:3 unless asked.

## copy

- 2026-10-02 "sounds like ai" about a hero line with three adjectives. cut to one plain sentence.

## the rule you keep repeating

- less. "cut anything that doesn't tell me something", said three times on three screens.
```

the refs that paid off for this person, kept here because they are taste, not method:

- high craft indie app pages, textures behind ui, pages alive without reveal on scroll.
- liquid glass done well on the web, for chrome only.
- the minimal pro tools people already trust (calm greys, one radius, weight 500 headings with negative tracking, 8 percent hairlines) when the ask is "clean, professional".
- simpler pages, aligned boxes, a readable round font, clean on first sight, for any dashboard.
